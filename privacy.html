Foilcase — Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

Foilcase is a collection tracker for trading cards. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics. This page describes exactly what the app does with data, including the parts that are easy to leave out.

What stays on your device

Your collection — which cards you own, what you paid, condition, grading, notes and any purchase dates — is stored in a database on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photographs taken while scanning are processed on the device by Apple's Vision framework and are never saved or transmitted. Nothing leaves the camera.

The app keeps a local diagnostics log of measurements from scanning attempts — distances, timings and whether a guess was corrected. It contains no images and never leaves the device. You can clear it in the app.

Network requests the app makes

The app makes three kinds of request, none of which carries anything about you — no identifier, no account, no description of your collection:

  • Card images, fetched from the public content delivery network that hosts the catalog's artwork.
  • Price files, fetched from a fixed address on GitHub Pages. The same file is served to everyone; the request does not say which cards you own.
  • The App Store, through Apple's StoreKit, to show the subscription and check whether yours is active.

As with any request over the internet, the servers involved — operated by their own companies, not by us — can see the network address it came from. That is a property of the internet rather than something the app sends.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. The app never sees, receives or stores a card number, billing address or any other payment detail. Manage or cancel a subscription in the App Store; cancelling takes effect at the end of the period already paid for.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to sell.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous versions remain in the public history of the repository this page is published from.
